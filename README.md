# Exploration of AI Use Case: Case Study on Self-Driving Cars

## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

## THEORY

Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, use Artificial Intelligence to operate with reduced or no human intervention, depending on their level of automation. They combine sensors, cameras, radar, LiDAR, machine learning, and computer vision to understand their surroundings and make driving decisions.

AI technologies used in self-driving cars:

1. Computer Vision: Analyzes camera images to identify roads, traffic signs, pedestrians, vehicles, and lane markings.
2. Machine Learning: Learns patterns from driving data to improve object recognition and driving decisions.
3. Sensor Fusion: Combines information from cameras, radar, LiDAR, and other sensors to create a more reliable understanding of the surroundings.
4. Decision-Making Algorithms: Determine when to accelerate, brake, change lanes, or turn based on traffic conditions.
5. Path Planning and Navigation: Calculates suitable routes and selects safe paths while avoiding obstacles.
6. Real-Time Processing: Processes sensor data quickly so the vehicle can respond to changing road conditions.

Case study: Waymo self-driving cars

Waymo develops autonomous driving technology that uses AI to detect nearby vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, and road obstacles. Its vehicles combine sensor data with perception, prediction, and planning systems to navigate roads and respond to traffic situations. This technology supports autonomous ride-hailing services in selected areas.

Despite these advancements, self-driving cars face challenges such as unpredictable road conditions, bad weather, sensor limitations, and unusual traffic situations. Therefore, safety testing and reliable system design are essential.

## RESULT

The study explains how Artificial Intelligence enables self-driving cars to perceive their surroundings, recognize obstacles, make driving decisions, and navigate roads. AI technologies improve driving automation and may help reduce human error, but ensuring safety and reliability under different road conditions remains a major challenge.
